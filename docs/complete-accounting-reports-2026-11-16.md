# Complete-account reports and printed statements

Reports now default to “الحسابات كاملة”, combining historical monthly settlements, the historical exceptional collection summary and new receipts; expenses similarly combine the saved historical total and new expense entries. The historical block identifies these amounts as included detail. “حركة فترة محددة” retains date-limited genuine cash movement reporting and excludes old settlements even when their dates overlap.

One pure accounting model drives the screen and printable statement. Imported exceptional settlements are not counted twice: the building historical aggregate is authoritative for overall income, while each apartment’s historical linked settlements remain in its paid amount. Legacy unassigned exceptional history is explicitly disclosed in the printed statement rather than invented as per-apartment shares. Aggregated old expenses appear as a clearly labelled undated summary row in complete-mode printouts.

Recorded treasury cash remains independent of historical income minus historical expenses. Opening cash is derived by removing genuine post-registration movement from the current balance; complete closing cash reconciles opening cash and new movement only. Period closing balances exclude later genuine movements. Shared typed selection passes directly to print preview, is bound to the building, and clears on close; old browser session-storage ranges cannot silently select a different print scope. Changing buildings resets the report scope.

Real Chromium at 390 and 1280 widths tests complete and period reports, per-apartment old/new payments, preview totals, and generated print HTML captured at the native printer bridge. Genuine new receipt 350.25 and expense 50.25 change treasury by 300 while retaining correct old totals. Existing historical-ledger regression flows cover mobile/desktop financial editing and residents.

No database, customer balance or payment record is changed. Printer integration was exercised through its bridge and generated HTML, not on a physical Android device or paper printer.
